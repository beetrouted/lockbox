Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026.

The short version

Lockbox & Key doesn't collect anything about you. That covers your files, your passwords, your name, your contacts, and how you use the app.

What stays on your device

  • Your vaults: locked files, kept where you choose. Even their names are locked.
  • Your People list: the names and Lockbox addresses you add, also locked.
  • Your Lockbox key: sealed by your device's security chip.
  • Your settings.

None of it is sent to us, because Lockbox & Key has no server to send it to.

What we receive

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no crash reports that send data out, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party code that could collect data.

When you send a file

It travels through whatever you choose, such as Mail, WhatsApp or AirDrop, so their privacy policies apply to that trip. The file stays locked the whole way: they can see its name and size, not what's inside.

Your vaults in a cloud folder

If you keep vaults in iCloud Drive, in Lockbox & Key's own folder there or any other, or in another synced folder, that service stores the locked files in your own account. We can't see them. It can see their sizes and when they change, nothing more.

Face ID and Touch ID

Apple handles these on your device. Lockbox & Key never sees your face or fingerprint data; it only learns whether the check passed.

Permissions

The app only uses the files you pick or drop in, and asks for Face ID or Touch ID only when opening something that needs it.

Children

Lockbox & Key collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its date change too.
